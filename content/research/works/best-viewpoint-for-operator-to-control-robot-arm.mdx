---
title: Best viewpoint for operator to control robot arm
category: robotics-for-nuclear-application
---
For the decommissioning of the Fukushima Daiichi nuclear power plant, we need to retrieve the nuclear fuel debris that remains inside the primary containment. One approach would be to use the teleoperation of a robotic arm. The viewpoints provided to the operator are carefully selected to achieve efficient teleoperation. Therefore, we are woking on viewpoint selection for robot teleoperation. Please refer to the papers [here](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9708809) and [here](https://www.robot.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/~yamashita/paper/B/B294Final.pdf) for further explanation.
